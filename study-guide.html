01. Shaped Entropy
Why pure random() gets boring

Iteration 1 has zero memory between notes: if everything is unpredictable, nothing surprises. Blending noise() and random() creates expectation first, then breaks it.

02. Deterministic Indeterminacy
Chance at design time, fixed in playback

Randomness chooses the notes while writing the algorithm, then randomSeed(0) and noiseSeed(0) freeze that exact run so both class playbacks sound identical.

03. Harmonic Safety Net
Just-intonation pentatonic ratios

[1, 1.125, 1.25, 1.5, 1.67, 2] drops dissonant semitones and tritones, so overlapping reverb chords stay musical even at 100% chaos.
